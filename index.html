Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Magnetar is a BitTorrent client for Android published by Green Cube in two editions: Magnetar (free, supported by advertising) and Magnetar Pro (paid, no advertising). This policy explains what data the apps handle and what they deliberately do not do. It applies to both editions unless stated otherwise.

Summary

  • We do not operate any servers. We do not have user accounts. We never receive your data.
  • All app data (search history, subscriptions, download queue, settings) stays on your device.
  • The free edition shows ads served by Google AdMob, which receives limited device data as described by Google's own policies. Magnetar Pro contains no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • BitTorrent is a peer-to-peer protocol: when you download a torrent, your IP address is visible to other peers. This is inherent to the protocol, not something we control.

Data stored on your device

The following information is stored locally in the app's private storage and is never transmitted to us:

  • Search history (you can clear it in the app).
  • Your keyword subscriptions and their match history.
  • The download queue and resume data for incomplete torrents.
  • Settings such as proxy configuration and bandwidth limits.

Search results are fetched directly from public torrent index sites you search against (currently The Pirate Bay via its public API and TorrentDownload). Your search queries go to those sites, not to us. Uninstalling the app removes all of the above.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — to contact torrent peers, trackers and search sites, and to honour your Wi-Fi-only preference.
  • Notifications — download progress and subscription match alerts (optional, you can deny).
  • Storage via the system folder picker (SAF) — to save downloaded files only into the folder you explicitly choose.
  • Run at startup — so subscriptions keep working after a reboot.

Advertising (free edition only)

Magnetar (free) uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display banner advertisements. To do so, Google may collect and process device identifiers, approximate location (IP-derived), app usage and advertising identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, under Google's Advertising policies. You can opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (Delete advertising ID). Magnetar Pro contains no ads SDK and makes no such processing.

Peer-to-peer traffic

BitTorrent transfers connect your device directly to other users. Your public IP address and the fact that you share a particular torrent are visible to those peers and to anyone monitoring the network. Please only download content you have the right to obtain; you are responsible for what you download and share with Magnetar.

Backups

Backups are created only when you request them and are written as a zip archive into a MagnetarBackup folder inside your chosen download folder. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, since no personal information is collected by us at all.

Changes to this policy

If the apps' behaviour changes, this policy will be updated here with a new "last updated" date.
